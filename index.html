MyMoney: Retirement Boleh Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

The short version. Your information stays on your own phone. We do not collect it, we cannot see it, and we do not sell it. The app has no account, no advertising and no tracking.

1. Who we are

MyMoney is a retirement planning app for people in Malaysia, made by Rachel Kee (“we”, “us”). You can reach me at rachelkeepl.work@gmail.com.

The app gives estimates to help you plan. It is not financial, tax or legal advice.

2. Information you type into the app

The app keeps what you enter so that it can work out your plan. This can include:

  • your age, retirement age and the age you want your money to last to;
  • your assets, such as EPF, savings, shares, property and crypto, with their amounts;
  • your income and spending, and the growth and inflation rates you choose;
  • in the People page: names, relationships, phone numbers and notes of people you may want your family to call, and a list of important papers with where they are kept and who knows.

All of this is stored only on your phone, inside the app. It is not sent to us or to any server of ours. We never see it.

We ask you not to type passwords or bank account numbers into the app.

3. Information the app asks your phone for

  • Contacts (optional). If you tap “Choose from my contacts” in the People page, the app opens your phone’s contact picker, or asks your permission to read contacts. Only the name and phone number of the contact you choose are copied into the app. The rest of your contacts are not kept or sent anywhere. You can say no and type the details yourself.
  • Clipboard and sharing. When you tap Copy or Share in Ask AI, the app puts text on your clipboard or opens your phone’s share sheet. The app does not read what is on your clipboard.
  • Fingerprint or face (optional). If you turn it on, your phone checks your fingerprint or face itself. The app never receives your fingerprint or face data.
  • Internet. Used for the price lookups described below.

4. What leaves your phone, and when

  • Exchange rates. To value assets held in other currencies, the app asks Bank Negara Malaysia’s public service for exchange rates. The request does not contain your information.
  • Crypto prices. If you add a coin, the app asks a crypto price service (currently CoinGecko) for the price of that coin. The request contains the coin name only.
  • Technical details. Like any internet request, these services can see your phone’s internet address (IP address) and basic technical details. They have their own privacy policies.
  • Ask AI. The app writes a question using your numbers, at the level of detail you choose (rounded, shares only, or exact). It never includes names, phone numbers, where your papers are kept, or account numbers. The text leaves your phone only if you copy or share it and paste it into an AI service such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. That company then receives it and handles it under its own policy. Please read what you are sending before you paste it.
  • Backups. If you make a backup, the app creates a file locked with a password you choose. You decide where to keep or send it. We cannot open it and we cannot recover the password.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or have access to your personal information.
  • We do not show advertising and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or rent any information.

If we ever add something that changes this, for example crash reports, we will update this page and the app first.

6. Keeping your information safe

  • The app can be locked with a PIN, and with your fingerprint or face. The PIN is kept on your phone only, in a scrambled form, and is not part of a backup. A forgotten PIN cannot be recovered. You would need to erase the app’s data and restore your own backup.
  • The PIN keeps other people out of the app. The saved information is not scrambled on the phone itself, so please keep your phone’s own screen lock turned on.
  • Keep your backup file and its password somewhere safe, and separate from each other.

7. Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your phone until you delete it. You can remove it by uninstalling the app, clearing the app’s data in your phone settings, or erasing it from the lock screen. Backup files you made, and anything you pasted into an AI service, are outside the app. Please delete those yourself.

8. Children

The app is made for adults. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will change the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app. Please check this page from time to time.

10. Contact

If you have a question about this policy or about your information, write to rachelkeepl.work@gmail.com. We will use your email only to reply to you.
